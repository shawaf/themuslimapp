## Full description  (≤ 4000 chars)

The Muslim App is your all-in-one Islamic companion — beautifully designed, Arabic-first,
and completely free. Everything you need for your day of worship, in one calm, elegant app.

🕌 PRAYER TIMES & ADHAN
Accurate daily prayer times for your location, with the next prayer and a live countdown
always in view. Enable the Adhan notification for each prayer so you never miss a salah.

📱 HOME-SCREEN WIDGET
Add the widget to your home screen to see today's prayer times and the time remaining
until the next salah at a glance — no need to even open the app.

🧭 QIBLA COMPASS
Find the direction of the Kaaba instantly with a precise, easy-to-read compass.

📿 AZKAR & TASBIH COUNTER
Browse adhkar by category — After Salah, Morning, Evening, Sleep, Travel and more — then
tap to count through each dhikr with a smooth digital tasbih. Progress is tracked for every
dhikr in the set, with authentic references.

🤲 DUAS
A curated collection of authentic supplications for every occasion, with Arabic text,
transliteration and translation.

📖 HADITH LIBRARY
Read and reflect on a selection of authentic hadith.

🗓️ HIJRI CALENDAR
Follow the Islamic (Hijri) date and this year's notable days and events, with the nearest
upcoming event highlighted.

🌙 DESIGNED FOR YOU
• Arabic by default (full right-to-left), with one-tap switch to English
• Light and dark themes
• A clean, modern design across every screen
• Lightweight, fast, and works offline for the core features
• No ads, no clutter

May Allah accept your worship. بارك الله فيكم
